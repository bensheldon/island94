The romance genre allows for a lot of nuance on how readers respond to scenes of sexual violence — we use terms like “noncon” (short for “nonconsensual sex,” with the related dubcon for “dubious consent”) or “forced seduction” (if you’re an elder millennial who remembers romance forums on the internet circa 2006.) These are terms for fiction; they do not apply to real life rape, alleged or otherwise. To see a journalistic piece making the same demurral about an alleged crime was wildly inappropriate and, in my view, worth calling out. Hence: The repost.
