> Liu is so absurdly limited in his understanding of anyone that isn't a Boomer/Gen X Chinese man that none of the societal reactions he depicts ring true for me. I found it telling how, despite all the teasing and set up, the books are ultimately uninterested in examining Trisolaris as a society and its apparent contradictions; Liu simply doesn't care about such things when they're not in service of reactionary whining.
