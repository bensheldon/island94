> There’s also a thought experiment in the work of philosopher Robert Brandom that feels analogous to what gets lost with new fully-AI generated and verified results in fields like math, CS, stats, etc. He imagines a community where people acquire beliefs about the world through reliable processes, but they can’t provide any reasons for what they believe. If they knew that their inferences were arrived at reliably, that itself would be a kind of rationale, so they cannot know that. He asks whether such a community could have knowledge. He concludes that they cannot, despite their true beliefs about the world, because with no ability to give and ask for reasons, they can’t even have the concept of reliability.
