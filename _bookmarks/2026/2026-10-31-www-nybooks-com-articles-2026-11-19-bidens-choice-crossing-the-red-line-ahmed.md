> At first I read that as an unintentionally tragicomic description of the asymmetry of power: while his former colleagues ran the US government, Paul was writing LinkedIn posts. Yet perhaps that misreads the nature of power itself. Because if all you do in government is operate imperial machinery to a predetermined end, what power do you really have? Sure, someone like Paul could not directly influence outcomes once he left government. But he was acting on his own belief about what is right and what is wrong, and there’s a different kind of power in that.

We like to think that “exceptionalism” describes a virtuous nation with an indispensable global responsibility.
