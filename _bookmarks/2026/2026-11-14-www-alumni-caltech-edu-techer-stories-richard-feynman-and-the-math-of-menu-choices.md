> “Feynman said this in a lot of cases: The first thing you’ve got to do is clearly define what you’re trying to do,” Leighton remembers. In the case of the restaurant dilemma, he says, “I didn’t define it clearly—I still haven’t even today—I was just trying to describe my behavior. But Feynman defined the problem in a way that suited him and was solvable.”
