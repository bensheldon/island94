> He goes into detail on his side project: the Domain Language Surveyor. The idea is to be able to examine different repositories, and analyze the vocabulary used inside those repositories to see if they are related bounded contexts. To do this he built a pipeline of tools, illustrating how a useful system blends both computational and inferential components. He then used this pipeline to analyze the vocabulary of some open-source healthcare codebases, to see how their bounded contexts related.
