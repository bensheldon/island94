The Daily WTF: Curious Perversions in Information Technology

https://thedailywtf.com/articles/comments/how-not-to-get-honey-trapped

November 24, 2026

I think that people’s attitudes to secrets are a great measure of how mature they are in their career.

We often have incredible levels of access, and there six stages of being handling the secrets that access gives us:

I have access to everything! This is cool!
I have access to everything! FEAR ME!
I have access to everything. Much of this is stuff I shouldn’t have access to. This requires responsibility and ethics.
I have access to everything. I wish I didn’t - secrets are a burden.
Is that a secret you have? I swear, if you bring that thing near me without good cause, I will END you.
Retirement or death.
Stage two is optional, and you either grow out of it very quickly or cease tobe employed. Or, in extreme circumstances, cease to be alive.

The later stages reflect the increasing realisation that secrets are not cool. They require considerable mental energy to hide. You have to constantly watch yourself to make sure that your replies in conversation or email don’t even hint at what you know, but are also typical of your normal communications patterns. It can be quite the draining experience.

Secrets suck. They are not cool. They are a burden.
