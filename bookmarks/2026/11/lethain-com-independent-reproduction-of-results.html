Shaping the industry through independent reproduction of results within our companies

https://lethain.com/independent-reproduction-of-results/

November 28, 2026

My professional goal at this point in my life is cultivating, and participating within, environments where I and folks I admire are able to do the best work of our lives. That’s hard to do! It’s even harder to do in a company that’s highly exposed to a bubble, because tenure matters a lot when it comes to doing excellent work. Technical context is relatively transferable, but domain context is not, and quality is the intersection of both. There’s a chance of doing extraordinary work in a bubble, but bubbles breed mercenary behavior, with substantial jumping across roles. There’s nothing inherently wrong with being mercenary–which mostly means looking out for yourself–but it’s at odds with the sort of durably excellent environment I am striving to create.
