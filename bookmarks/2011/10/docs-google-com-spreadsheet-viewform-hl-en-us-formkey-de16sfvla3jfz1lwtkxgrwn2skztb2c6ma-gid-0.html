I wrote:

> “Please don’t destroy the wonderful community of friends and authors that share their Google Reader feeds with me, and I with them. While I understand Google’s desire to recreate that community in Google+, this is the wrong way to do so. I fear that what makes Google Reader special, and causes me to return day after day, will be lost forever. “
